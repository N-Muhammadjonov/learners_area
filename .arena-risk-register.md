is either **cleared by evidence** or **confirmed and fixed**. Cleared lines are
kept (not deleted) so the same suspicion is not re-investigated from scratch.

## Confirmed and fixed

- **(XC) Admission review could approve the same request twice.** Confirmed
  2026-09-26 by firing two approvals in parallel: the read-then-write sequence let
  both callers pass the "still pending?" check, producing two status writes, two
  notifications and two audit entries. Fixed in `src/lib/data/admission.ts` by
  claiming the decision with a conditional update
  (`where id = … and status = 'pending' … returning id`); the loser now gets
  `already_decided`. Regression test: `scripts/verify-admission-race.mjs`
  (6 checks: one 200 and one 409, one status row, one audit row, one notification,
  the account becomes ACTIVE, and a later attempt gets 409).

- **(XK) Intermittent production crash on `/reading` (digest 1594744578).**
  Confirmed and fixed 2026-09-26. The connection pool had no ceiling in time
  (`new Pool({ connectionString })`: no `connectionTimeoutMillis`, no
  `statement_timeout`, no `query_timeout`, 10 connections per instance) and
  `/reading` asked the database twelve times in five sequential waves, one of
  them a full scan of `reading_questions`. Under a pooler at its client limit
  that is either a hard failure (`Failed query: … [cause] Connection terminated
  unexpectedly`) or a request that never returns. Reproduced with a
  120 ms-per-hop proxy and a backend cap: before — 0/12 requests rendered
  (4 at a time) and one request hung past 15 s; after — 12/12 rendered, 0
  refusals. Fix: bounded pool + server statement timeout, and the reading page
  reduced to 7 queries in 3 waves. Evidence and the production test:
  `docs/READING-PRODUCTION-STABILITY.md`, rig `reading-stability.mjs` 32/32.
  Production verification is the owner's run (EXTERNALLY UNVERIFIED here).
