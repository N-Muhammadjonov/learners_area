### A2-01 | The Lost Umbrella
Tom takes the number 12 bus to work every morning. Last Tuesday it was raining, so he took his big green umbrella. The bus was very full, and Tom stood near the door. When he got off at his stop, he forgot the umbrella on the bus. He was wet and unhappy all day. In the evening, he called the bus company. A kind woman answered the phone and asked about the colour of the umbrella. She told him that a driver had found it. The next day, Tom went to the bus station after work and got his umbrella back. Now he always checks his seat before he leaves the bus.

At Hill Street School, the children have a small garden behind the library. Every class looks after one part of it. Class four grows tomatoes, and class five grows potatoes and carrots. In summer, the school is closed, so some parents come on Saturdays to water the plants. In September, the children pick the vegetables. The school cook uses them to make soup for lunch. Last year the soup was so popular that the cook wrote the recipe on a big poster in the dining room.

### A2-05 | A Day at the Zoo
On Saturday, Amir visited the city zoo with his class. The bus left school at nine o'clock. First, they saw the elephants. A zookeeper got the hat back with a banana. At lunch, they ate sandwiches next to the lake. In the afternoon, Amir's favourite part was the penguins, because they swam very fast. ### A2-07 | The Cat in the Tree
One windy afternoon, Mrs Patel heard a strange sound from her garden. She looked out of the window and saw her cat, Coco, high in an old apple tree. Coco was crying because she could not come down. Mrs Patel was too old to climb the tree, so she called her neighbour, a young man named David. David brought a long ladder from his garage. He climbed slowly, and Coco jumped into his arms. Mrs Patel was so happy that she made David a big plate of biscuits. Since that day, Coco sits on David's wall every morning and waits for him to say hello.

### A2-08 | A Rainy Holiday
In August, Ben and his parents went camping in the mountains for a week. They wanted to walk, swim in the lake and cook outside. But it rained for five days; their tent was small and everything inside it was wet. On the third day, they found a little museum in the next village. It had old farm tools and a room about the history of cheese. Ben thought it would be boring, but he loved it. They also played cards for hours in a café. On the last two days, the sun came out and they finally swam in the lake. Ben says it was his best holiday.

### A2-11 | Breakfast in Different Countries
People eat very different breakfasts around the world. In England, a traditional breakfast has eggs, sausages, beans and toast, but most people only eat it at the weekend. In Japan, many families eat rice, fish and miso soup in the morning. In Turkey, breakfast is often a big meal with bread, cheese, olives, tomatoes and tea. In many parts of Mexico, people like eggs with spicy sauce and tortillas. Some people do not eat breakfast at all, because they are not hungry or they are in a hurry. Doctors say that a good breakfast gives us energy for the morning and helps children to think better at school.
